# Game Pillars: Retro Hop

## Jump Feel First

Movement quality is the central feature. Any addition that weakens response,
clarity, or control is cut or deferred.

## Fair On First Sight

The player should understand the cause of every success and failure. Layout,
color, and spacing teach the rules before punishment.

## Small But Complete

The project ships one finished level rather than a broad unfinished framework.

## Original Retro Spirit

The game can use simple shapes, bright colors, tile-like surfaces, and tight
challenge. It cannot copy protected characters, enemies, props, music, level
beats, sprites, names, or branding.
