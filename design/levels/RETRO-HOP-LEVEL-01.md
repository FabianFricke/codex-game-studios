# Level 01: Meadow Run

## Intent

Meadow Run is a short introductory level with a complete arc: safe movement,
basic jump, optional gems, first hazard, raised platforming, second hazard, and
finish gate.

## Beat Map

1. Safe starting platform teaches movement.
2. Low gem line teaches collection.
3. Small gap teaches jump commitment.
4. Step platforms teach vertical movement.
5. First walker teaches hazard timing.
6. Raised gem line rewards controlled jumps.
7. Wider platform and second walker combine timing and spacing.
8. Final platform leads to finish gate.

## Layout Rules

- No blind jumps.
- No required enemy stomp.
- Every gem is reachable.
- Hazards are red and visibly distinct.
- Finish gate is green and visually distinct.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Critical path can be cleared without collecting gems.
- All gems can be collected in one run.
- The player can see the next required landing area before jumping.
- The level can be completed within two minutes by a practiced player.
