# Game Brief: Retro Hop

## High Concept

Retro Hop is a compact one-level platformer about crossing a bright obstacle
course with clean jumps, readable hazards, optional gems, and a satisfying
finish.

## Player Fantasy

The player is a small nimble traveler sprinting through a colorful toy-world
course, reading danger quickly and mastering the route through timing.

## Pillars

- **Jump Feel First**: movement response is more important than feature count.
- **Readable On Sight**: the player can tell what is solid, collectible,
  dangerous, and final without instruction text.
- **Short Level, Real Arc**: the level has an intro, a middle challenge, a
  final combined challenge, and a clean finish.
- **Original Retro Spirit**: use simple shapes, bold color, and immediate
  feedback without copying protected characters, layouts, props, sprites,
  sounds, or branding.

## Audience

Players who enjoy short, skill-forward 2D platformers with clear rules and
low-friction replay.

## Target Platform

Windows desktop.

## Engine

Unity 6000.4.8f1.
