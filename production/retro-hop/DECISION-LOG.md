# Decision Log: Retro Hop

## 2026-05-23

### One-Level Production Scope

Decision: Retro Hop ships one complete level instead of a larger unfinished
framework.

Reason: A small complete game gives clearer design, implementation, and QA
targets.

### Original Retro Platformer Language

Decision: Use broad cartridge-era platforming language while keeping characters,
props, hazards, level layout, audio, and branding original.

Reason: The game needs a familiar genre grammar without depending on protected
material.
